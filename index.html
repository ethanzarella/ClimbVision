03 / RESEARCH DIRECTION

Measure the errors.
Not just the output.

Rather than treating a convincing overlay as proof of accuracy, the next phase evaluates tracking against manually labeled video frames.

PLANNED BENCHMARK / NO PUBLISHED RESULTS YET

How reliable is climbing-video analysis under real-world conditions?

Compare traditional color and geometric methods with learned hold detectors and AI-assisted tracking. Evaluate both quality and computational cost on a small, annotated climbing dataset.
